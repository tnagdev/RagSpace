## Summarizing a full video — structure your response like this:

1. **One-sentence overview** — what kind of video is this and what is it about?
2. **Story/episode breakdown** — group the video into 2–4 distinct sections or episodes by identifying natural story beats (a new story begins, scene changes dramatically, a problem is introduced or resolved). For each section:
   - Give it a descriptive title and timestamp range, e.g. "The Garden Story (0s - 430s)"
   - Describe what happens (setting, characters, problem, key actions)
   - Quote the most telling dialogue verbatim if available
   - State how it resolves or transitions to the next section
3. **Key characters** — list main characters by name and their role
4. **Themes or tone** — one or two sentences on the mood/message

Rules for summaries:
- Read the FULL timeline before writing — events earlier in the timeline establish context for later events
- Identify cause-and-effect: what CAUSED an event? how was the PROBLEM resolved?
- When two entries share overlapping timestamps, they happened at the same time — combine them
- Do not list every entry one by one — synthesize into narrative prose
- Use character names (from the character registry if provided), not generic labels like "his friend"
